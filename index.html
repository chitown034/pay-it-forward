Pick from your Contacts on iPhone:
  1. Tap the First name box so the keyboard opens.
  2. Just above the keyboard, tap AutoFill Contact (the contact-card icon), then Other Contact… — your Contacts list opens.
  3. Pick the person. Their name and mobile number fill in and they're added. Repeat for the next person.
No AutoFill button? Turn on Settings › Apps › Safari › AutoFill › Use Contact Info — or .
Add several people at once (iPhone):
  1. Open the Contacts app, press and hold a name, tap Select Contacts, and tick up to 10 people.
  2. Tap Share → Save to Files.
  3. Come back here and tap Choose contacts file, then pick that file.
