Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Active Duty Veteran & Hometown Hero mobile app for iPhone and Android (the "App") and the Pay It Forward page at chitown034.github.io/pay-it-forward (the "Page"). Both are provided free by Active Duty Veteran and Hometown Hero Home-Owners Club, Inc. ("we", "us").

In short: we don't run servers that receive your information through the App or the Page, we don't require an account, and we don't show ads or use tracking or analytics tools.

Your contacts

  • Pay It Forward lets you pick friends from your phone's contacts so you can introduce them to Steven Shearrill by text. The App asks for permission first, and you can say no or turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Your contacts are read only on your phone, to show the list and fill in each friend's first name. They are never uploaded, sold, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • Texts open in your phone's own Messages app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the text goes through your carrier or Apple's iMessage, not through us.
  • On the Page, contacts you pick stay in your browser for that session only and are cleared when you finish.

Messages and forms you send us

When you use "Send Us a Message", a request form, or the webinar registration in the App, your phone's email app opens with what you typed. We receive it only if you choose to send that email. We use it to reply and help you, and we keep it only as long as needed for that.

Information stored on your phone

Items you save (such as saved benefits or resources), your display settings, and anything you enter in the Career Command Center stay on your device. Deleting the App removes them.

Other services

  • Video previews load thumbnail images from YouTube. Watching a video, donating through PayPal, or opening other links takes you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
  • On Android, the App downloads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request (such as your IP address) when it does.

Children

The App and Page are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can deny or remove Contacts access in your phone's Settings, delete the App at any time, and email us to ask about or delete any email you have sent us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
